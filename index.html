Materials & Metrology 2026

Thermal Smoothing (Annealing) of FDM 3D-Printed PLA

Problem: FDM PLA parts suffer from high surface roughness (Ra) yet warp easily above Tg.
Action: Tested 36 roughness samples (ISO 4288) and 6 tensile bars (ISO 527-2) across 60°C–120°C.
Result: Identified 80°C / 30 min as optimal (−4.59% Ra with only −4.1% UTS trade-off).
